# Six-Month Content Plan (30 Articles)

**Rules:** an Eco Pak specialist writes or technically reviews every article, and the article carries a named author with qualifications. Cite primary sources: EPA Punjab notifications, PEQS/NEQS gazette, the Act. Don't publish turnaround times, prices or certification claims unless they are verified. Each article links to its service page and ends with a CTA (call / WhatsApp / quote form).

**Suggested URL pattern:** `/insights/<slug>`. To publish one, add a route and an entry in `src/seo/routes.js` with `pageType: 'Article'`. Schema: Article + BreadcrumbList.

Abbreviations: SP = service page link, Inf = informational, Com = commercial investigation.
